# Audience: Acme Waterproofing (demo company)

## Market

B2B only. Commercial and industrial projects across the UAE. Residential is out of scope.

## Primary buyers

| Buyer | What they care about |
|---|---|
| Developers (project and asset teams) | Handover without defects, warranty cover, no post-completion disputes |
| Main contractors | Programme, coordination with other trades, clean sign-off |
| Facilities and asset managers | Leaks in occupied buildings, repair history, maintenance cost |
| Consultants and project managers | Specification, test results, material approvals |

Typical building types: warehouses and logistics sites, retail and mixed-use podiums, office towers, hotels, factories, car parks.

## Their biggest fear

**A leak after handover.** Once a building is handed over, a leak means a warranty claim, a disrupted tenant and a reputation hit. Everything else (delay, cost, warranty wording) matters, but it sits behind this one.

Secondary worries: delays on the critical path, cost overruns from rework, and warranties that look strong but are hard to claim.

## What they need to hear

- How the work is diagnosed, specified and tested, in plain steps.
- What the warranty covers and what it does not.
- Which systems and materials are used, and why they suit UAE conditions (heat, humidity, sudden heavy rain, high water tables near the coast).
- That Acme finds problems early with diagnostics, not after handover.

## Positioning (what makes Acme different)

1. **Warranty:** clear, written cover that buyers can understand.
2. **Systems and materials:** the right system for each element (roof, basement, podium, tank), explained.
3. **Diagnostics:** moisture surveys and leak tracing before and after the work.

This is the demo positioning chosen by Amit. It is not a claim about any real firm.
