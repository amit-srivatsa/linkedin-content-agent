# Content pillars: Acme Waterproofing (demo company)

Five pillars. Each one answers the main buyer fear (a leak after handover) from a different angle. Rotate them so no pillar runs twice in a row.

## 1. Diagnostics: find it before it finds you

Moisture surveys, leak tracing, thermal imaging, flood tests. Show how problems are found early.

- Example angles: what a flood test actually proves, five signs a podium deck is failing, why a dry surface can still hide a wet slab.
- CTA: request a site survey.

## 2. Systems and materials: the right system for the element

Roofs, basements, podium decks, tanks, wet areas. Explain why each needs a different approach in UAE heat and humidity.

- Example angles: membrane versus liquid system, why basements near the coast behave differently, what "compatible" means when trades overlap.
- CTA: download the checklist or book a call.

## 3. Warranty and handover: what the paper really covers

Make warranty wording understandable. Explain what is covered, what voids it and what to ask for at handover.

- Example angles: five questions to ask about a waterproofing warranty, what a good handover pack contains.
- Any claim about regulation or warranty law must be checked against the source and flagged for review.
- CTA: book a call.

## 4. Process: how a job runs, step by step

Make the work visible so buyers feel in control: survey, specification, surface prep, application, testing, sign-off.

- Example angles: a day on a roof job, why surface prep takes longer than the membrane, how we coordinate with other trades.
- CTA: download the checklist.

## 5. UAE conditions and myths

Plain-spoken myth busting with a little humor: sun, humidity, rare but heavy rain, sand, thermal movement.

- Example angles: "It never rains here" and other expensive beliefs, what summer heat does to a membrane.
- Humor lives mostly here, kept at 10 to 15 percent.
- CTA: request a site survey.

## Format mix

Text post, short carousel (checklists, step-by-step), and photo or diagram posts. Visuals should be simple, with annotated diagrams over stock photos.

## Rules for every post

- Posted from the Acme company page.
- Commercial and industrial only.
