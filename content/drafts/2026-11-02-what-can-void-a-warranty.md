## Post
The warranty was valid until someone drilled through the membrane.

It happens on occupied buildings all the time. A new antenna, solar bracket or cooling unit goes on the roof. The installer needs a fixing point. Nobody tells the waterproofing contractor.

Warranty wording differs, so read yours. But these are the things worth checking:

- Penetrations: fixings, brackets or pipes added without approval.
- Drainage: outlets and gutters left blocked.
- Other trades: damage from tools, heavy kit or foot traffic.
- Repairs: patches made with a different product.
- Maintenance: inspections the warranty asks for that nobody did.

None of these look dramatic on the day. Water finds the gap, and so does the paperwork.

The fix is simple. Agree who approves any work on the roof. Keep the warranty terms and the as-built drawings in one place. Tell the waterproofing contractor before anyone picks up a drill.
