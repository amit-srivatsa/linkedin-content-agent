## 1. What a flood test actually proves

- **Pillar:** Diagnostics
- **Reader:** Developer, consultant
- **Format:** Text post
- **Hook:** "The flood test passed on Tuesday. Here is what that does and does not tell you."
- **Angle:** Explain what a flood test shows (the membrane holds water at that moment, in that area) and what it cannot show (details, edges, later movement).
- **CTA:** Request a site survey
