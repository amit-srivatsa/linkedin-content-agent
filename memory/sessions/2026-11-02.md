### Decisions

- Placeholder figures are approved for this demo only. They live in `brand/audience.md` and every post using one must be tagged `[DEMO FIGURE]`. This is an exception to the `AGENTS.md` rule against invented metrics. `AGENTS.md` was not changed. No invented clients, projects or certifications.
- LinkedIn rules built into the writing skill: hook inside 140 characters, 150 to 300 words, one idea, one CTA, no body link by default (link in first comment), carousels of 6 to 10 slides, up to three hashtags, no em dashes, suggest staff reshare because company pages get less reach.
- LinkedIn evidence is mostly vendor blogs and the numbers conflict. Treat as directional and re-check quarterly. LinkedIn says it has no deliberate link penalty, while a third-party study found about 26.5% less reach for linked posts. We took the cautious side.
- Research skill grades claims A, B or C. Only grade A counts as fact, and regulation or warranty claims are flagged until confirmed.
- Nothing is ever published without Amit's review.
